The books

One row per imported account. Missing months mean missing statements — and missing statements mean transfers get miscounted as spending. munim doctor checks the same thing.

Settling accounts

Debits and credits across your own accounts, paired up. Auto-linked pairs are exact, unambiguous matches; the rest need your pick. Dismiss a transfer that will genuinely never have a counterpart (e.g. money sent to an untracked account) so it stops appearing here.

Waiting for your pen

Confirm a merchant once and every identical entry across the ledger is written up with it. People (blue) are only ever categorized by you. Tick several rows and assign one head at once to avoid repeated clicks.
